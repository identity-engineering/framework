# 03 – Decision Layers & Metabolization

**Status:** Draft (Phase 1)

## Why an Identity messages another

From the Identity’s own perspective:

1. To preserve or expand its own future causal options (Causal Entropic Forces).
2. To establish or strengthen Coupling (Frequency resonance / Biology Interactive).
3. To utilize another Identity’s Mass / Curvature (Gravitation).
4. To participate in Collective Emergence / Regulation (Biology Collective + Physics Collective).

Any message that cannot be justified under at least one of these is suspect and should be rejected by default policy.

## Decision Layers (evaluated in order)

1. **Transport / Discovery** – Can I reach the target Identity Card?
2. **Recognition** (Biology Interactive) – Does the target’s Recognition policy accept me (and this Signal type)?
3. **Ownership & Jurisdiction** – Am I allowed to send this Payload under the declared Ownership rules?
4. **Causal-Entropy Guard** – Does this message risk reducing the future freedom of the sender or receiver beyond declared constraints?
5. **Frequency Fit** (Physics Interactive) – Is the requested frequency compatible with both parties’ current tension fields?
6. **Acceptance** – Payload is delivered; Receipt is generated.

If any layer fails, the message stops. Only a Recognition or Delivery Receipt (with reason) is returned.

## Metabolization (Biology Single)

Once accepted, a message may trigger metabolization in the receiving Identity:

- Update State Differential (past → present)
- Adjust Vision Gradient (present → future) if the content is relevant
- Possibly change local Mass or Curvature (only with explicit consent or high-trust Coupling)
- Emit a Metabolization Receipt back to the sender (optional)

Metabolization is the concrete operationalization of the Biology-lens **Metabolism** concept (Single scale) for information. It is the process by which Interaction becomes relative geometry inside the receiving Identity.

## Feedback Loops

- Positive Frequency resonance → stronger Coupling, lower future Recognition threshold.
- Repeated mismatch → Damping (Physics Collective) or eventual blocking.
- Collective messages feed into Regulation and Specialization (Biology Collective).
