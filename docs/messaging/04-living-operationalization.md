# 04 – Living Operationalization (Metabolism & Multicellularity)

**Status:** Draft (Phase 1)

This document makes two previously conceptual Living-layer notions concrete for the Messaging Layer.

## Metabolism (information)

**Definition (operational):**  
The process by which an Identity takes in a Signal + (optional) Payload, transforms it according to its current Stem / Mass / Curvature, updates its internal state, and optionally produces output Signals or actions.

### Concrete steps inside the OS

1. **Ingestion** – Envelope accepted after Recognition.
2. **Classification** – Signal type + content-type decide which Operating Cycle hooks fire.
3. **Transformation** – Content is interpreted relative to the Identity’s current Vision Gradient and State Differential.
4. **State Update** – Optional, guarded change to local geometry.
5. **Emission** – Zero or more outgoing Signals / Messages / side-effects (tools, tasks, human notifications).

Metabolism is therefore the bridge between the Interactive layer (Signal, Coupling) and the Single-layer geometry of the receiving Identity.

## Multicellularity (collective messaging)

**Definition (operational):**  
A set of specialized Identities that remain distinct (each keeps its own Stem and Card) yet coordinate under a higher-order Collective Identity that has its own Card, Regulation rules, and Damping.

### Messaging implications

- Addressing a Collective Identity first hits the Collective Card.
- Regulation decides whether the message is fanned out, routed to a specialist, or handled centrally.
- Specialization determines which member Identity(ies) actually metabolize the Payload.
- Damping prevents oscillatory or runaway message loops inside the collective.
- Each member still retains the right to reject via its own Recognition policy.

This is the first concrete mechanism that turns the Collective-scale concepts (Emergence, Regulation, Specialization, Damping) into something the OS can execute.
